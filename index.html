Who I Am

About Me

"Committed to research-led teaching, student mentorship, and interdisciplinary academic development."

I am a B.Sc. (Engineering) graduate in Information & Communication Technology from Mawlana Bhashani Science and Technology University (MBSTU), and I am currently pursuing my MSc at the same institution. I serve as an Instructor in ICT & Office Applications & Programming at Bamundi Karigori Training Center, Gangni, Meherpur, where I deliver structured instruction and hands-on laboratory sessions while mentoring students toward professional readiness.

My research interests include Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, Cybersecurity, and Bioinformatics, with ongoing work aimed at publication in international venues. As Vice Chair of the IEEE MBSTU Student Branch, I contribute to organizing technical workshops and research seminars to support student development and academic engagement.

I am seeking a University Lecturer or Research position where I can contribute to research-led teaching and interdisciplinary academic advancement, bridging the gap between emerging AI technologies and practical, impactful education.
